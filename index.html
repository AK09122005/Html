Mukesh Ambani is a prominent Indian billionaire industrialist and the chairman and managing director of Reliance Industries Limited. Born on April 19, 1957, in Aden, Yemen, he took charge of expanding the family empire founded by his father, Dhirubhai Ambani. Under his leadership, Reliance has grown from a textile and petrochemicals business into a massive multinational conglomerate spanning oil refining, retail, media, and digital services. He famously revolutionized India's telecom sector by launching Reliance Jio, providing affordable 4G and digital connectivity to hundreds of millions of people. Consistently ranked among the wealthiest individuals in Asia and the world, he also owns the Mumbai Indians IPL cricket team and plays a major role in Indian philanthropy and business

Elon Musk, born on June 28, 1971, in Pretoria, South Africa, is a billionaire entrepreneur and innovator known for leading some of the world's most influential technology companies. He serves as the chief executive officer and lead designer of SpaceX, the CEO of Tesla, and the owner of the social media platform X (formerly Twitter). Having built early successes like Zip2 and PayPal, Musk has focused his career on revolutionizing electric vehicles, advancing private space exploration, and developing artificial intelligence ventures like xAI, becoming one of the wealthiest figures globally
